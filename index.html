In Dedication

This digital simulator is dedicated to Dr. Mary Whiton Calkins (1863–1930), the pioneering American psychologist who originally developed the mechanical memory drum and the paired-associate learning technique. Despite fulfilling every requirement for a Ph.D. with distinction, Harvard University consistently refused to grant her the degree solely on the basis of her gender. Her legacy is a lasting reminder that brilliant, necessary work exists entirely independent of institutional validation.
